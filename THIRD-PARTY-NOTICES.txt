Recorded acoustic piano and guitar notifications
-----------------------------------------------

The six WAV files under app/renderer/sounds are derived from instrument samples
distributed by Nicholaus P. Brosowsky in tonejs-instruments:
https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments

Samples: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
The complete licence is included in app/renderer/sounds/CC-BY-3.0.txt.
These recordings are not relicensed under Vibe Studio's MIT source-code licence.

The supplier's sample-source-info.txt identifies the original piano source as
VSCO 2 (Versilian Studios) and the acoustic guitar source as the University of
Iowa Electronic Music Studios. The supplier edited the recordings for silence,
ramps, level consistency, noise and pitch, as documented in its README.

Vibe Studio changes: original question/success/error note sequences, shortened
tails with fades, stereo 44100 Hz PCM16 WAV rendering and peak normalization.
No pitch shifting, instrument-synthesis replacement, commercial song or game
recording is used. Attribution is provided here without implying endorsement.
Source hashes, note sequences, processing and output hashes are retained in
app/renderer/sounds/recorded-manifest.json. Only the rendered notification clips,
not the upstream JavaScript library or its full sample collection, are bundled.
